ABOUT ASHAS CLOTHING

Crafting high-energy streetwear accessories, statement shades, and modern tribal jewellery.

OUR STORY

Accessorize Your Story with ASHAS™

Founded in Calicut (Kozhikode), Kerala, ASHAS CLOTHING is a boutique fashion and streetwear accessories label dedicated to individuality, edge, and contemporary urban aesthetics. From precision-crafted geometric sunglasses and retro-futuristic digital timepieces to handcrafted macramé tribal necklaces, Cuban links, and statement signet rings, every piece in our collection is curated to elevate personal style.

We operate our flagship walk-in retail boutique in Karuvissery, Kozhikode, along with an online storefront at ashasstore.in serving fashion-forward customers across all 28 states and union territories in India with reliable, trackable India Post Speed Post delivery.
