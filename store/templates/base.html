PRIVACY & PURCHASE POLICY
1. Privacy & Data Protection

ASHAS STORE respects your privacy. All customer information (name, phone number, delivery address) submitted during checkout is strictly protected and used exclusively to process your order, dispatch parcels via India Post / courier, and provide real-time tracking updates. We never share or sell personal data.

2. Strict No Refund, Replacement & Cashback Policy

All sales are final once orders are placed. Due to the exclusive limited editions and hygienic nature of our boutique apparel, jewelry, and eyewear:

  • No Refunds: Payment once completed is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • No Replacements / Exchanges: Products once dispatched or delivered cannot be exchanged or replaced.
  • No Cashback: We do not offer cashback, credits, or monetary compensation post-payment.
3. Shipping Rates & Delivery

Parcels are dispatched promptly from Kozhikode, Kerala via India Post Speed Post with live tracking:

  • Inside Kerala: ₹65 for orders containing Shades (protective eyewear packaging); ₹55 for other products.
  • Outside Kerala: ₹95 flat pan-India delivery.
4. Secured Payments

All transactions are protected by 256-bit SSL encryption via Razorpay. Your banking and payment credentials are never stored on our servers.
